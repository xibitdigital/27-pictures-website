Experiments

Interactive prototypes and visual studies from 27 Pictures — early darkroom work outside the main horror and cosplay production slate.

This lab is where we test narrative interfaces, AI-assisted illustration workflows, and cinematic reading experiences before they graduate into full productions like The Red Smile or our YouTube shorts.

Each experiment is self-contained, intentionally small, and built to explore one idea: motion, interaction, or visual language. New studies will appear here as they leave the darkroom.
