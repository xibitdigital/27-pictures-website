Interactive Toons

Interactive toons from 27 Pictures — illustrated stories you read rather than watch, with captions that speak and pages you turn.

An interactive toon is a FlipFrame story: black-and-white plates, voiced word balloons, and a reader that works like a book. We build them in this lab — the same darkroom as The Red Smile shorts and our cosplay productions — to test narrative interfaces before they graduate into a fuller production.

Each toon is self-contained and intentionally short. New titles appear here as they leave the darkroom.
