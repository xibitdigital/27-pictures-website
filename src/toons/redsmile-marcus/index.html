RED SMILE: Marcus

Episode 2 of RED SMILE

The second episode of the RED SMILE series: psychological horror in heavy black-and-white gekiga ink — crushed shadows, hand-inked plates, light that never quite reaches the corners. Halina cleans the tower every night and nobody in it knows her name. Marcus is pleasant to her, asks how she is, and waits for the answer. Behind him a window opens by itself.

Episode 1, RED SMILE: static, is complete — twelve plates. This one is in progress.

This is a FlipFrame reader: full-screen plates you turn like a book, with captions that read themselves aloud in English, Italian, German, French. It needs JavaScript — the pages and audio stream from our CDN as you read.
