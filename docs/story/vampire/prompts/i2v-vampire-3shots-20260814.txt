# Describe only: what moves, how the camera moves, ambience.

--- SHOT 1 — approach (wide) ---
She begins walking slowly toward camera, coat swinging open with each step, hair shifting, boots hitting wet pavement. Camera dollies backward at her pace, holding her full body centred. Rain drifts through the streetlight, reflections ripple on the wet asphalt, distant traffic lights flicker behind her. Steady, unhurried, cinematic.

--- SHOT 2 — boots (low tracking) ---
Low camera close to the wet ground, tracking sideways alongside her boots and the hem of her coat as she walks. Puddles splash lightly under each step, neon reflections streak across the water, coat leather creases and swings. Shallow depth of field, background lights pulled into moving bokeh. Smooth lateral dolly, constant speed.

--- SHOT 3 — the look (close-up) ---
She stops walking and turns her head to camera, holding eye contact, a slow faint smile forming at one corner of her mouth. Almost no body movement, hair settles, breath visible in the cold air. Camera pushes in very slowly on her face. Rain falls behind her, streetlight steady. Held, still, unsettling.
