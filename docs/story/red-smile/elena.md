# Elena

**Status: partly established.** Elena is the only character who actually exists
in a config — she is the whole cast of `redsmile-static`, episode 1, and her 13
caption lines are shipped in four languages. Her **mother was a society agent**, and Elena has been **inoculated as a vessel
— a host kept to kill with** (2026-08-19). Everything else here is a pitch.

## In one line

The woman episode 1 happens to — and, it turns out, not a stranger to any of
this: her mother was one of the society's placed agents.

## Appearance — established by the plates

**There is no character sheet for Elena and there should not be one.** She is
drawn across 12 published plates; those are the reference. Anything generated
later must be pinned against the plates themselves, not against prose.

From episode 1: dark hair worn up in a loose knot with strands escaping, seen
from behind for most of page 1; slight build; at home in soft clothes. The only
close study of her face in the episode is the final panel of page 1 — one eye,
enormous, with the sigil surfacing in the iris.

## Proposed — her mother

Elena's mother was a **placed agent**, the same tier as [Halina](halina.md):
an ordinary woman in an ordinary job, positioned where the society needed eyes,
reporting to the three and banishing nothing.

This single fact removes the thing that was weakest about episode 1 — that Elena
was chosen at random — and replaces it with something much worse. Three readings,
in rising order of cruelty:

- **Inheritance.** The entity found her the way it found her mother, because the
  household was already in its field of view. Nobody targeted anyone. It simply
  came back to an address.
- **Retaliation.** Her mother cost it something, and it waited. A thing that
  moves at the speed of transmission can afford to be patient with a daughter —
  and turning an agent's child into the weapon it kills agents with is precisely
  the kind of symmetry it would choose.
- **Recruitment.** Elena was being watched by the society, not the entity — being
  considered, the way her mother once was — and it reached her first. The three
  were the reason she was on anybody's list at all.

**Elena almost certainly does not know**, and never got the chance to. That is
the version to write. Her
mother kept a night job and told her nothing, because that is what the tier is
for, and Elena grew up thinking her mother cleaned offices, or filed records, or
drove a van.

## Proposed — what it costs the society

The three now have a dead or missing agent's daughter as episode 1's host. Every
one of them will read that differently:

- **Viktor** has watched this happen for three centuries and will be the one who
  says the useful, unbearable thing about it.
- **Adaeze** was also saved out of a fight she did not choose. Elena is the
  version where nobody arrived.
- **Tokiro** carried one person out once. He will be measuring himself against
  this for the rest of the series.

**Halina is the sharpest connection available.** Two placed agents, both women,
one older — it is entirely natural that she knew Elena's mother. If she did, then
the woman cleaning Marcus's offices at 2am already knows exactly what this thing
does to the families of people like her, and has stayed anyway.

## Open questions

1. **Is her mother dead, taken, or missing?** Taken is the strongest: a host who
   was never recovered, and possibly still walking around.
2. Did Halina know her?
3. Elena is a vessel, so episode 1 does not end with her death — it ends with
   her being ready. What is she sent to do, and to whom?
4. Who was the text from — a partner, and where is that person now?
5. Did her mother leave anything behind in the flat? The society would want it.
