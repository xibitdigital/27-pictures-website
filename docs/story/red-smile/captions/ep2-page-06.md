## The idea

**This is the page where the cleaner turns out to have been placed there.** The
cart is her cover and her only weapon — `halina.md` is explicit that nobody
equipped a junior agent — so she fights with a trolley and a spray bottle and it
works well enough to buy seconds.

**The last line changes what the page is.** He is on one knee, blind, and he finds
her name: _"…Mrs Nowak."_ Page 5 ended on _"Mrs…?"_, trailing off into the blank
where a name should be. Nobody in that building knows what she is called — that is
the asymmetry ep 1 set up — and the thing in him supplies it anyway. So the page is
not "she got away", it is "she got away and it learned her name". This is also
where the surname enters canon; it is deliberately not said any earlier.

**She keeps the formal register while shoving a cart at him.** The Italian is
`Stia indietro` (lei), not `stai`; the French `Reculez`, not `recule`. A woman who
has called him _sir_ all night does not switch to informal here, and only the
translations can carry that.
