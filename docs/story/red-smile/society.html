Sub Signo

Under the sign. Three people hunt a thing that lives in transmission. Two of the three are partly what they hunt, and the one who is not is the one who does the killing.

The name

Under the sign, and under the standards

The society is named for the mark its members carry, not for its absence. Viktor and Adaeze wear the unicursal hexagram on their skin — the entity's own shape, worn deliberately as ward and anchor. They work beneath it in both senses: protected by it, and subject to it.

Sub signis is also the Latin for troops serving together under their unit's colours — the oldest way to say that these people are one body, without pretending three of them and a cleaner amount to an institution.

Tokiro is the exception that proves it. He is sub signo by membership and refuses to be sub signo on his body: he will not carry the thing's picture on his skin, and goes into rooms unprotected because of it. The name says what the society is; he is the standing argument about what that should cost.

The top tier

Three principals

Founded by Viktor in the 1940s, after he escaped an attack by the thing it now hunts. Not an inherited order and nobody above him: one survivor's answer to what he watched happen in Berlin in 1934, when the entity got into the heads of the first television audiences.

Not an agency and not an army. The qualification for the top is unteachable, so nobody is promoted into it — there are three, and there have only ever been three.

Scholar

Viktor

Half vampire · 350 years old · looks sixty

Occultist and master banisher. He closes a case by knowing the name. Three and a half centuries of watching the same things surface, be put down, and surface again wearing whatever medium the century provided — which is worth more than any of the rest of it.

Finder

Adaeze

Half demon · early forties · human mother, demon father

She locates and reaches. Tokiro saved her during the battle with her father, which is the founding fact of her life and the reason she is in this work. She has never entirely settled whether that was mercy or recruitment.

Blade

Tokiro

Akuma banisher · late thirties · entirely human

He ends it in the room. The only one of the three who is simply a man — he banishes akuma for a living and carried one home, and he notices that without resenting it.

It does not get to recruit its way out of being three.

Below them

Placed agents

Ordinary people in ordinary jobs, positioned where the society needs eyes. They watch, they report, they banish nothing. They are deniable, unprotected, and the thing they watch for kills them specifically.

Agent

Halina

The cleaner · mid fifties · undercover in Marcus's building

Her cover is close to perfect, and not because it is clever — it is perfect because it is true. She really does the job, every night, properly, for the wage. Nobody in that building has ever looked at her long enough to wonder.

Agent · former

Elena's mother

Dead, taken, or missing

She worked this tier too. Which means the society has been losing these people for a while, and that the cost of the work does not stop at the person doing it.

What they hunt

It has no hands, so it makes them

The entity moves through media — radio, television, computers — and crosses into a person by way of an image. Not the sound, not the wiring: the picture. To see it is to be exposed, which is why this whole series is drawn rather than filmed. It was in the heads of the first television users in 1934; every decade since has handed it a wider aperture.

It knows it is being hunted, and it kills society agents because it feels threatened by them. But it cannot cross a room. So it takes civilians and keeps them as vessels, and the killing is done by someone who was a neighbour a fortnight ago.

Who What happens to them
A civilian Taken and inoculated. The image crosses, the sigil surfaces in the iris — and what is grown in them is a weapon.
A society agent Killed. Not hosted, not crossed into. Removed — and removed by a vessel, not by anything with a shape.
The smile is not what is left of a person. It is what a finished weapon looks like.

Outside the society

The people it has already reached

Vessel

Elena

Episode one · an agent's daughter, which is why she was never arbitrary

A text from her partner confirms she is alone. The television finds a channel that should not exist. Then the same sigil surfaces inside her iris. Episode one is not a prologue — it is the transmission.

Vessel

Marcus

Chief executive · late thirties · owns the screens

The entity did not need to corrupt the man who runs the largest image-delivery apparatus ever built. It only needed him to look at one. He has a society agent cleaning his offices, and neither of them knows about the other.

The boundary matters. Marcus and Elena are victims, not members — and being on the payroll is not immunity either. Each episode is a host.

The mark

Wearing the thing's own picture

Viktor and Adaeze both carry the unicursal hexagram tattooed on their skin — his on the back of the bare right hand, hers inside the left wrist. It is the same sigil that fills the television in episode one.

If the sigil is the transfer vector, wearing it deliberately is either suicidal or the whole trick. The proposal: it is inoculation and anchor — you mark yourself with the thing's own shape so the image has nowhere new to arrive, and so you can look at it directly and act.

Tokiro refuses it. He will not carry the thing's picture on his body, and he goes into rooms unprotected because of it. Neither man has convinced the other in years of trying.

Unresolved

Open questions

  • What is the society called, if anything — and do its members ever say it aloud?
  • Is Adaeze's father dead? Tokiro fought him; nobody has said who won.
  • How does the entity identify an agent? The answer decides how Halina gets caught.
  • Why is Halina still alive — is the cover working, or is it waiting to see who she reports to?
  • Is there anything of Elena left inside the thing that used to be her?
  • Do the wards actually work? Viktor and Adaeze are the two it has most reason to kill.
Status. Established: the three principals and their natures; the agent tier; the entity's medium, its image vector, that it kills agents, and that Elena and Marcus are vessels. Everything else on this page is a proposal — a pitch to accept, edit or throw out.
