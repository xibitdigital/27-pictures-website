## In one line

An akuma banisher in a good suit, the society's blade — and the man who carried

## Appearance — locked

Matches the character sheet prompt. Binding.

A Japanese man, **late 30s**. Tall, wiry, athletic, with the still upright
balance of a swordsman. Serious unsmiling face, hard flat gaze, strong straight
brows, sharp jaw. Long black hair pulled into a **high tight ponytail**, temples
smooth, a few loose strands at the face.

**The scar.** One continuous old scar from the **left** side of the forehead,
down through the eyebrow, past the outer corner of the left eye, ending on the
left cheekbone. Raised, pale, unmissable. **Left side in every single view** — a
scar that changes sides between views makes the sheet useless as a reference.
The left eye is intact and undamaged; do not let it drift into a blind or milky
eye.

**Costume.** Well dressed and deliberately formal: dark tailored three-piece
suit, high-collared shirt, no tie, under a long dark haori-style overcoat falling
to mid-calf and hanging open. Traditional split-toe boots.

Modern-formal rather than traditional dress, on purpose — it keeps him distinct
from Viktor's Victorian and Adaeze's contemporary tailoring while the weapons
still read as old.

**Weapons.** A **daisho** at the left hip: katana and shorter wakizashi, plain
dark lacquered scabbards, thrust through a wide dark sash tied over the suit.
Wrapped hilts, simple round tsuba. **Sheathed in every view** on the sheet — a
turnaround is not an action pose.

**No mark.** No tattoo, no hexagram, no star, no sigil, anywhere. His two
colleagues both carry the unicursal hexagram; he refuses it, and the absence is
characterisation — see *Who he is*. Worth stating explicitly in any prompt: if
Viktor's or Adaeze's sheets are used as style references, the mark leaks across
by association.

## Sub Signo

One of three principals of [Sub Signo](the-society.md), the secret society that
hunts dark entities. He is its **blade** — the one who ends a thing when knowing its name is
not enough.

## Who he is

A banisher of akuma, and the most literal member of the three: Viktor argues a
thing out of the world, Adaeze finds it, Tokiro goes into the room.

**He killed Adaeze's father, and carried her out of the same room.** He went in
to end a demon and did; walking out with its daughter was not the assignment. It is the most
important thing he has ever done and he has never once described it as mercy —
he made a judgement in a moment and has spent the years since living beside the
result, watching her become the best of them.

That decision is also the crack in him. He banishes akuma for a living and
brought one home, and the society contains a half-vampire and a half-demon while
he remains the only one of the three who is simply a man. He does not resent it.
He notices it.

**No mark, and it is a choice.** Viktor and Adaeze both carry the unicursal
hexagram. Tokiro refuses it — and the society is called *Sub Signo*, **under the sign**,
which makes him a member who declines the one thing the name is about. He has
pointed that out more than once, and Viktor has never enjoyed it. His argument is that a banisher who wears the sigil
has already conceded something; Viktor's counter-argument is that it is only a
tool. Neither has convinced the other in years of trying.

The suit is not vanity. He works in other people's homes on the worst night of
their lives, and dressing properly is a courtesy to them.

## How to use him

- **He arrives when talking has failed.** Structurally the third act.
- **The scar before the swords.** A formal, controlled man who happens to be
  armed — not a warrior first.
- **Draw the swords sheathed** unless the beat genuinely requires otherwise. A
  drawn blade should mean the situation has already gone wrong.
- **He and Adaeze are the series' engine.** The man who saved her is the man who
  killed her father, and whose whole profession says she should not exist. All
  three of those are true at once and none of them is in dispute between them.
