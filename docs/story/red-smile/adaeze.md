## In one line

Half-daemon by birth, hunter by choice — the one who finds the thing, and the
only member of the three who is partly what they hunt.

## Appearance — locked

Matches the character sheet prompt. Binding.

A Black African woman, **early 40s**, beautiful, tall, athletic, composed. Broad
nose bridge, full lips, strong jaw, high forehead. Natural hair in tight dark
coils, cropped close at the sides and fuller on top.

**Rendering her skin is a craft note, not a detail.** In pure black-and-white
ink her deep skin tone must be a rich **mid-grey** held with cross-hatching, with
a clean white rim light along cheekbone, nose and jaw — never flat black, never
so dark the features are lost. Get this wrong and the character disappears into
the plate.

**Light eyes.** Very pale irises, almost colourless, reading bright against the
dark ink. Her defining feature; clearly visible in every view.

**Half-daemon, and restrained with it.** Faint dark veining spreading from the
outer corners of the eyes; slightly elongated canines, visible only when she
smiles; two small blunt horn buds barely breaking the hairline. No wings, no
tail, no claws, no gore. The restraint is the point — she passes.

**Costume.** Modern and sharp, deliberately not period: tailored charcoal wool
coat to the knee worn open, fitted dark polo neck, slim trousers, low boots.

**The mark.** Small unicursal hexagram tattooed **inside the left wrist** — same
order as Viktor, deliberately a different placement. He wears his on the back of
the hand where it is seen. She wears hers where it is shown only on purpose.

## Sub Signo

One of three principals of [Sub Signo](the-society.md), the secret society that
hunts dark entities. She is the one who **finds** them — the field operator, and the only
member who is partly what they hunt.

## Who she is

**Human mother, demon father.** Not a curse she carries and not a secret she
apologises for: it is simply her parentage, and half of it tried to kill her.

Tokiro **killed her father, and saved her in the same fight.** That is the founding fact
of her life and the reason she is in this work at all. She was on the wrong side of a fight she had not chosen, and a stranger with a
sword made two decisions in the same minute: end the demon, keep the daughter.
She has never entirely settled whether the second was mercy or recruitment.

**He killed her father in front of her.** That is not a wound she nurses and not
a debt she disputes — her father was a thing that had to be stopped, and she has
said so. But it sits under every conversation the two of them have, permanently,
and neither of them has ever raised it.

Her father is the shape of every case she takes. He is dead; Tokiro killed him.
That is settled, and it is the reason she works.

**The demon half is an instrument.** It is why she can stand in a room a normal
operator could not, why she notices a wrongness three streets away, and why the
society's reach is wider than three people should manage. It is also the reason
Viktor watches her a fraction longer than he watches anyone else, being partly a
monster himself and knowing exactly how that arithmetic works.

She passes completely. The tells — the veining, the canines, the horn buds — are
for the panel where the reader has already relaxed.

## How to use her

- **She is the one who talks.** Viktor's menace is silence and Tokiro's is
  stillness; hers is fluency. She can hold a page of dialogue and stay
  frightening.
- **She is the way in.** Contemporary, articulate, at ease — the member a
  civilian like Elena would actually open a door to.
- **Her half is a cost, not a cheat.** If it solves a problem in one panel, it
  should take something from her in the next.
- **She and Tokiro have history, not romance** — or not yet. He saved her; that
  is a debt, an intimacy and a slight resentment all at once.

## Voice

No `adaeze` entry in `scripts/jax-voices.json` yet.

Direction: warm, articulate, unhurried — a voice it is pleasant to be talked to
by. The horror is the content, never the delivery. Eleven v3, moderate
stability; `[softly]` and `[laughs]` sparingly. **Never** the `ai`/`badai`
metallic chain — she is not a machine and not a monster on the soundtrack.
