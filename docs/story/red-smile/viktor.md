## In one line

Three hundred and fifty years old, half vampire, dressed for 1880, and the man
the other two send for when nobody knows what the thing in the room actually is.

## Appearance — locked

Matches the character sheet. Binding.

Tall and markedly imposing — roughly **8.5 head-heights**, the length in the legs
and torso, **shoulders broad and square**, and lean with it. Not a heavy man; a
wide, long one. The overcoat hangs off those shoulders and falls straight, so at
any distance he is a tall dark column with a hat on top.

Gaunt face. High sharp cheekbones, deep vertical lines beside the mouth,
swept-back silver hair, close-trimmed silver beard.

**Snake eyes.** Pale irises, vertical slit pupils, reptilian. His single most
important feature and the one thing a plate may never lose to shadow — if the
lighting eats his eyes, it is the wrong lighting.

**Costume.** Full-length Victorian winter overcoat, heavy dark wool,
double-breasted, velvet collar, calf-length. Waistcoat, high collar, cravat.
Black leather gloves **carried, not worn** — the right hand stays bare on
purpose. Tall black silk 1800s top hat.

**The mark.** Back of the **right** hand, tattooed black: the unicursal hexagram
(see [README](README.md)). The one hard link between him and what happens to
Elena, so it is drawn exactly or not at all.

## Sub Signo

Viktor **founded** [Sub Signo](the-society.md). He is its occultist and senior
banisher — the one who knows what a thing is, what it wants, and which words end
it — and the only member who was there at the beginning, because he *is* the
beginning.

### 1934, Berlin

He was living in Germany when television arrived, which is where television
arrived first: the world's earliest regular service, a handful of receivers,
audiences small enough to count.

He watched [the entity](the-entity.md) get into the heads of those first users.
Not a rumour that reached him later — he was in the city while it happened,
already two and a half centuries old and well practised at noticing when
something is wrong with the people around him.

**It attacked him, and he escaped.** During the war, which is as much as the
bible fixes: he got away from something that does not usually let anyone get
away, and he has been building against it ever since.

Sub Signo exists because of that escape. Not an ancient order he inherited — a
society **he started in the 1940s**, after surviving the thing it hunts.

**Proposed — why he survived.** He is half vampire. The entity crosses into a
human host by way of an image, and he is not entirely one. Whatever it reached
for in him did not take, or did not take properly — and that failure is the seed
of everything the society knows: if a crossing can fail, a crossing can be
**prevented**. The unicursal hexagram his members wear is the engineered version
of an accident that happened to him in a German city ninety years ago.

That reading also makes the society's method personal. Every ward is a copy of
Viktor's own body.

## Who he is

**Three hundred and fifty years old, and half vampire.** Born around 1676. The
human half is why he still eats dinner and keeps a barber; the other half is why
there has been time to read everything.

He looks sixty because sixty is where he stopped. The Victorian dress is not
affectation or nostalgia — it is simply the last era in which he felt current,
and by then he was already a hundred and eighty. A man who has outlived the
fashions of six generations stops chasing the seventh.

The long life is the qualification, not a superpower. He has seen the same
entities surface, be put down, and surface again wearing the technology of the
day. **He recognises things** — and in this one case he does not have to
recognise it at all, because he was standing there in 1934 when it started. That
is worth more than any of the rest of it, and it is why the society is three
people rather than an army.

He is a **master of occultism and a banisher** — the scholar half of the work.
Where Tokiro closes a case with a sword, Viktor closes it by knowing the name.

**Half vampire, and the story should cost him for it.** Suggested: he does not
hunt people and has not for centuries, but the hunger is not gone, it is
governed. A society that fights dark entities containing a man who is partly one
is the most interesting thing about him, and Tokiro — a banisher of akuma — has
to have made peace with it, or not quite.

## Established — he feels an agent die

**Episode 2 ends with Viktor.** Not in the building, not in the fight — somewhere
else entirely, when the feeling reaches him that [Halina](halina.md) is no longer
alive.

He does not see it, is not told, and arrives far too late to matter. He simply
**knows**, and comes.

This is the right size of power for him: not a weapon, not a rescue, just an
awareness that costs him something and saves nobody. Three and a half centuries
of keeping people in place, and what he gets for it is the moment each one stops.

**Staging it:** the reader has just watched Marcus kill her. Cut to Viktor, still,
somewhere quiet, and let him lift his head. Say almost nothing. He should not
explain what he is feeling — the reader already knows, and is a scene ahead of
him for once.

**Do not make it convenient.** If he can feel an agent die he cannot feel one in
danger, or the tier would never lose anybody. It arrives after, always, and only
for people he placed himself.

## How to use him

- **Never as a jump scare.** The horror in a Viktor panel is never him. He is the
  calm in it.
- **The hand before the face.** A plate can introduce him entirely through the
  bare right hand and its mark, the rest unlit.
- **He is the exposition, so keep him short.** He knows what the thing is; let
  him say it in one line and be wrong once, memorably.
- Speaks rarely. Never raises his voice.
- **He carries the count.** He has felt this happen before, more times than he
  will say, and Elena's mother is probably one of them.
