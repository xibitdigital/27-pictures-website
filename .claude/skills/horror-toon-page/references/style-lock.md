# Horror toon-page — style lock (compact)

Use inside Seedream **text-to-image** prompts. Keep the live prompt short; this file is the expanded reference.

## Ink & value

- Pure black and white manga / gekiga ink
- Crushed shadow pools; sparse white for form edges and eyes
- Cross-hatching and grainy screentone; avoid soft gray watercolor wash
- High contrast; light is scarce and unfriendly

## Atmosphere palette (pick 1–2 per page)

- Wet night alley, rain on black asphalt
- Clinical fluorescent corridor / morgue cold
- Occult residue, ritual chalk, wrong geometry
- Domestic space made hostile (kitchen, hallway, basement)
- Body-horror restraint: one clear mutation if asked, not a pile of gore

## Composition

- Vertical page **1008×1792**, three **horizontal** stacked panels
- Thick black gutters, black outer border
- TOP establish dread → MID intrusion → BOTTOM payoff / ECU
- Negative space can be pure black; do not fill every corner

## Avoid

- Cyberpunk neon, teal-orange grade, photoreal 3D
- Cute / shōnen sparkle eyes as default
- Speech balloons, captions, SFX lettering, logos, watermarks
- Diagonal slash panels, triangle crops
- Extra limbs (unless the beat states one mutation)
