# Erin & the Goblins — character & world lock

Derived from the shipped plates in `cdn-backup/toons/erin/assets/`
(27 pages, 1152×1728, B&W except the cover).

## Erin

**Sheet plate:** `c363b4d490e8b574c61f92716bdc7dd4.jpg` ("ERIN — PREQUEL ERA":
full body, head detail, angry profile inset). Attach it as ref Image 1 on every
generation.

- Teenage girl, wiry athletic, average height, pale skin
- Short choppy dark bob, straight fringe over the brows, spiky side tufts;
  slightly lighter grey tone in the ink than her clothes
- Large sharp eyes, heavy lashes, thick angled brows; default expression is a
  hard glare, mouth set or bared in a snarl when fighting
- Black zip-up track jacket: high collar, full-length zip, ribbed cuffs and hem
- Black trousers with a thin pale side stripe
- Black lace-up combat boots, chunky sole
- Fights bare-handed on interior pages; the cover shows twin curved daggers —
  only draw blades if the beat asks
- Rendered with a white rim light so the black outfit reads against dark
  backgrounds

**Anti-drift:** never long hair, never a ponytail, never a skirt or dress,
never armour, cape or hood, never colour, never a different jacket. Same
outfit every page of the prequel.

## Goblins (pack)

**Ref plates:** `b19084ec3aa6db1355311aea48adfd23.jpg` (alley pack chase),
`6bfcd46718a6a8defd3b22e4998c7ddf.jpg` (colour cover crowd).

- Waist-height hunched creatures, long sinewy arms, knuckles near the ground
- Warty knobbed cranium with small horn-like nubs, long pointed ears
- Bulging pale eyes with pinprick pupils, wide mouth full of crooked fangs
- Ragged loincloths / scraps of cloth; knotted wooden clubs, crude blades
- Move in packs of 4–8, always from shadow, always grinning
- Grey-green skin — reads as mid-grey screentone in B&W

**Anti-drift:** not orcs, not trolls, not humanoid warriors, no full armour, no
firearms.

## Goblin King

**Ref plate:** `1f45add7e288635d156651e19f9122b9.jpg`.

- Twice Erin's mass, broad shoulders, same goblin face scaled up and brutal
- Jagged bone/iron crown, long pale hair
- Heavy scaled plate armour, spiked pauldrons, wide belt
- Throne-cave setting: stalactites, rubble, torch shadows

## Supporting

- **Sophia** — the missing child; blonde, smiling, seen in newspaper photos
  and posters (`646efa4c…`). Draw her as a printed photo, not a live figure,
  until the story says otherwise
- **Silhouetted traffickers** — faceless coated figures, black fill only
- **The black van** — plain windowless panel van, midnight street

## World

- Crumbling medieval-slum town: timber-and-plaster houses, steep tiled roofs,
  church spire on the skyline, cobbled lanes, brick alleys, hanging wires
- Erin's room: cracked plaster walls papered with notes, maps, sigils and
  clippings; bare hanging bulb; iron bed; desk buried in newspapers and books
- Portal beat: white rip of light with a spiral vortex, papers blown into the
  air (`1f45add7…` is the boss; the portal plate is `0fc4824c…`)
- Underground: goblin tunnels, crates, cave halls, stalactites, torchlight
- Night is the default; the moon is the usual key light

## Style

- Crisp black ink linework, dense cross-hatching, grey screentone and ink wash
- Deep solid blacks, harsh white highlights, strong figure-ground separation
- Speed lines for motion, radial burst lines for impact
- Thin white gutters, thin black keylines, white page margin
- Menace without gore — this is a young-reader dark fantasy

## Lettering
